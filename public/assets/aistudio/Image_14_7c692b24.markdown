## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an immersive, high-stakes aerospace command center tuned for intense competitive focus. It approaches the grueling preparation for JEE/IIT not as academic drudgery, but as a deliberate space flight campaign: high-agency, focused, and deeply aspirational.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Genre:** Cinematic Sci-Fi HUD meets Precision Productivity.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep vacuum space punctuated by controlled high-luminosity neon telemetry, luminescent glass telemetry slabs, and directional energy accents.
- **Personality:** Decisive, vigilant, brotherly/sisterly mentorship. It demands discipline while removing friction, celebrating grit, and maintaining clear trajectory visualization.
- **Visual Tenets:** Heavy atmospheric depth, translucent layered glass cards, hairline luminescent strokes, and purposeful status beacons that direct attention without causing cognitive fatigue.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict dark-mode equilibrium to reduce eye fatigue during late-night problem-solving cycles while maintaining dynamic contrast ratios.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#7C5CFF` - Electric Violet):** Drives primary momentum, active state triggers, progress arcs, and dominant focal points.
- **Secondary (`#22D3EE` - Cyber Cyan):** Represents active telemetry, real-time metrics, live timers, and technical instrumentation.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB020` - Mission Gold):** Reserved for apex milestones, daily mission targets, rank badges, streak multipliers, and vital alerts.
- **Base Canvas (`#0A0E1F` to `#0E132B`):** Deep cosmic navy gradient that anchors depth without cold pitch-black harshness.
- **Surfaces (`#131938` at 60% opacity):** Translucent frosted chassis for modules, control banks, and feeds.
- **Hairline Radiance Borders:** Defined by `#2A3362` for structural containment, upgrading to `#7C5CFF33` or `#22D3EE33` for active states.
- **Typography Tokens:**
  - `Text-Emphasized`: `#FFFFFF` for hero displays, headers, and core metrics.
  - `Text-Standard`: `#CBD5E1` for prompts, problem texts, and active guidance.
  - `Text-Muted`: `#94A3B8` for field labels, timestamps, and secondary metadata.
  - `Text-Subtle`: `#64748B` for inactive counters and structural captions.

## Typography

The type system balances technical punch with maximum reading endurance.

- **Space Grotesk (Headings, Telemetry, System Labels):** Provides an assertive, technical aesthetic reminiscent of flight control displays. Use uppercase formatting paired with generous letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for status indicators, rank counters, and operational directives.
- **Inter (Body, Complex Questions, Explanations):** Serves as the functional counterweight. High x-height and clear apertures ensure that complex mathematical formulas, physics problems, and mentor feedback remain legible under prolonged focus.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture transitions from an agile, thumbs-first interface on mobile to an expansive multi-cockpit operations dashboard on desktop screens.

### Grid & Layout Strategy
- **Mobile (Base to 767px):** Single-column stacked stream. Margin: `1rem`, element gaps: `0.75rem` to `1rem`. Mission telemetry sits in fixed top micro-bars; primary call-to-actions pin to bottom safety zones.
- **Tablet (768px to 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margin: `1.5rem`, Gutter: `1.25rem`. Two-column modules split daily objectives from revision logs.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid layout with a persistent 280px left flight-nav rail. Margin: `2.5rem`, Gutter: `2rem`. Supports three visible operational zones: Flight Plan (schedule), Live Cockpit (active timer/problem solver), and Telemetry (accuracy metrics and rank ladder).
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Maintain strict internal vertical cadence using multiples of `0.25rem` (`4px`). Use `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) within cards to maintain breathing room around high-density data.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, muddy drop shadows. Instead, it relies on physical light transmission: luminous background glows, optical glass density, and directional edge illumination.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Deep Space Void):** The root background canvas (`#0A0E1F` to `#0E132B`). Contains subtle chromatic mesh orbs that drift gently beneath the UI to suggest ambient energy.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Chassis / Dock):** `background: rgba(19, 25, 56, 0.45)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, edged with a solid 1px hairline border of `rgba(42, 51, 98, 0.6)`. Used for secondary widgets and passive cards.
- **Level 2 (Active Mission Containers):** `background: rgba(19, 25, 56, 0.70)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, framed with `1px solid rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.25)`. Features a 120px radial highlight positioned at the top-right corner (`rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Level 3 (Apex Floating & Flight Prompts):** Modals, test timers, and milestone banners. `background: rgba(19, 25, 56, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`, framed by `1px solid rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.4)`. Highlighted by a directional glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 30px -5px rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature combines generous structural container curves with tight, tactical inner elements.

- **Primary Cards & Mission Modules:** Set to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px` to `28px`), giving large glass panels the aerodynamic feel of modern flight instrumentation.
- **Inner Controls, Inputs, and Mini-Widgets:** Set to `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) or `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to preserve usable real estate and create clear parent-child nesting contrast.
- **Interaction Targets (Buttons, Badges, Status Beacons):** Fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) to create high-priority touchpoints that clearly stand out against orthogonal module panels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Thrust (Action Button):** Pill-shaped, gradient-filled (`linear-gradient(135deg, #7C5CFF 0%, #633BF3 100%)`). Text in `#FFFFFF`, `label-lg`. Framed by a subtle top-lit inner border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`). Hover: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.45)`, scale to `1.02`.
- **Secondary Telemetry Button:** Pill-shaped, transparent glass backing (`rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.08)`), framed with `1px solid rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.4)`. Text in `#22D3EE`. Hover: `background: rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.16)`.
- **Apex Milestone / Mission Start:** Pill-shaped, gradient-filled (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FFB020 0%, #F59E0B 100%)`). Black bold typography (`#0A0E1F`) for immediate, unmistakable urgency.

### Cards & Mission Slabs
- **Daily Mission Directive (Hero Card):** Glass container (`24px` radius) bordered with a gradient stroke that shifts from `#FFB020` to transparent. Contains a subtle amber orb glow in the header. Houses current syllabus targets, IIT-JEE countdown telemetry, and a primary action trigger.
- **Problem Workspace Card:** Neutral glass module (`rgba(19, 25, 56, 0.6)`) with a low-contrast hairline stroke (`#2A3362`). Code/formula snippets render inside deep recessed insets (`#0A0E1F`) with rounded-lg edges.

### Status Indicators & Mission Badges
- **Status Beacons:** Ultra-compact pill chips (`padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`).
  - Active: `#22D3EE` text, `rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.12)` background, accompanied by a pulsing 6px neon dot.
  - Critical/Urgent: `#FFB020` text, `rgba(255, 176, 32, 0.15)` background.
  - Locked: `#64748B` text, `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.1)` background.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Terminal:** Flat cosmic surface (`rgba(10, 14, 31, 0.75)`) encased in a `1px` stroke of `#2A3362`. Height `48px`, `rounded-lg`. Typography is crisp white Inter, with placeholder text in `#64748B`. Focus state transitions the border to `#7C5CFF` and adds a soft violet glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 92, 255, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls
- **Radio Buttons / Options Selection:** Custom circular nodes with a 1px border (`#2A3362`). When selected, shifts to an active cyan boundary with an electric center: `border-color: #22D3EE`, fill: `#0E132B`, center: 8px solid `#22D3EE` glowing orb.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (`6px` radius). Checked state transitions to `#7C5CFF` with a crisp white check icon and a localized border sheen.

### Progress & Telemetry Arcs
- **Trajectory Meters:** Background track uses a neutral dark stroke (`#1E264F`). The active progress bar features an electric linear gradient (`#7C5CFF` through `#22D3EE`) with a blurred point light source resting at the leading tip to communicate dynamic forward motion.
